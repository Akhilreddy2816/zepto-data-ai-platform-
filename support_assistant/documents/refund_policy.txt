# Zepto Customer & Delivery Refund Policy

## 1. Quick-Commerce Refund Guarantee
- If delivered products are damaged, expired, missing, or delivered past the 15-minute SLA guarantee by over 30 minutes, customers are eligible for a 100% instant refund or Zepto Cash credit.
- Refund requests must be logged in the Zepto App within 48 hours of delivery order timestamp with item photos attached.

## 2. Eligible Items for Instant Refund
- Perishable groceries (Fruits, Vegetables, Dairy, Bakery) with quality defects or spoilages.
- Wrong item delivered or missing items from sealed bag.

## 3. Non-Refundable Categories
- Opened personal care items, cosmetics, or hygiene products unless damaged prior to seal breakage.
- Electronic gift vouchers or third-party digital code purchases.

## 4. Processing Timelines
- Zepto Wallet / Cash Refunds: Processed within 10 minutes of claim approval.
- Original Payment Method (Credit Card / UPI / NetBanking): Processed within 3-5 business days depending on the customer's bank.
