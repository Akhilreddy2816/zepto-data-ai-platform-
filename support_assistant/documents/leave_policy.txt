# Zepto Annual Leave & Absence Policy

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlement
- Paid Casual/Sick Leave (SL/CL): 12 days per calendar year (accrued 1 day per month).
- Privilege / Earned Leave (EL): 18 days per calendar year.
- National Public Holidays: 10 fixed company holidays per year.

## 2. Special Leave Categories
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks paid leave for female employees.
- Paternity Leave: 2 weeks paid leave following childbirth.
- Bereavement Leave: Up to 5 consecutive days of paid leave for immediate family members.

## 3. Leave Application Rules
- EL requests of more than 3 consecutive days must be applied at least 14 days in advance in HR portal.
- Sick leaves exceeding 2 consecutive days require a certified medical practitioner doctor certificate.
