# Zepto Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

## 1. Introduction & Core Values
Welcome to Zepto! We are committed to deliver speed, reliability, and excellence across quick-commerce operations. Every team member is expected to uphold integrity, customer obsession, and extreme ownership.

## 2. Work Hours & Attendance
- Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, Monday through Friday.
- Flexible work policy allows up to 2 days of remote work per week for engineering and tech teams.
- Core attendance hours are 10:30 AM to 4:30 PM.

## 3. Code of Conduct & Anti-Harassment
Zepto strictly enforces a zero-tolerance policy against harassment, discrimination, or non-inclusive behavior. Concerns should be escalated immediately to hr-escalation@zepto.in or the POSH committee.

## 4. Equipment & Data Security
- Company-issued laptops must be secured with full disk encryption and BitLocker enabled.
- Never store production credentials or customer PII in unencrypted local text files.
- VPN must be connected when accessing internal APIs or database bastions remotely.
