# Zepto Human Resources (HR) Policy

## 1. Onboarding & Probation
- All full-time hires undergo a standard 3-month probation period.
- Performance confirmation reviews occur in week 11 with manager feedback and HR approval.

## 2. Learning & Professional Development Allowance
- Every employee receives an annual INR 50,000 learning stipend for certifications, tech conferences, and books.
- Prior approval from functional line managers is required before claiming reimbursement via Darwinbox.

## 3. Appraisal & Bonus Cycle
- Annual performance reviews run from April to May.
- Variable performance bonuses are disbursed in June based on individual performance ratings (Scale 1 to 5) and company EBITDA milestones.
