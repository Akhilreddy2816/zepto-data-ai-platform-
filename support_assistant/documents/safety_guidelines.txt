# Zepto Dark Store & Delivery Rider Safety Guidelines

## 1. Dark Store Operational Safety
- Protective footwear and high-visibility vests must be worn inside warehouse fulfillment centers at all times.
- Heavy machinery or electric pallet trucks must only be operated by certified store personnel.
- Fire exits, emergency aisles, and fire extinguishers must remain unobstructed 24/7.

## 2. Delivery Rider Road Safety
- Delivery partners MUST wear ISI-marked helmets while riding motorized two-wheelers.
- Maximum speed limits (40 km/h in city lanes) must be obeyed regardless of delivery ETA deadlines. Zepto does NOT penalize riders for traffic delays.
- Night deliveries require reflective raincoats and operational front/rear lights.
